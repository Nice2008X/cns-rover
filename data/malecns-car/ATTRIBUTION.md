# MaleCNS car circuit attribution

MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Google Research, and collaborators.

CNS Car transformations: visual/central subgraph extraction, normalized
unsigned weights, engineered visual sectors and rate dynamics, and a new
car readout. No Fly Brain Codex assets. Not biologically validated.
